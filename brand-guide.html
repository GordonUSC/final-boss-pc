Identity system

Six marks, one character. Every mark is the same boss ball seen at a different distance.

03

Construction

The boss ball is built from one circle. Everything else, horns, crown, eyes, is a ratio of that radius. The wordmark is drawn on centerlines and stroked, so letters and numerals share one pen.

04

Clearspace and minimum size

Give the boss room to breathe: one crown height on every side. Below 48 px the horns and seams blur, so the small mark takes over, and at 16 px the pixel boss does.

Primary 48 px or 0.75 in

Wordmark 128 px or 1.5 in wide

Roundel 36 px or 0.5 in, embroidery 0.75 in

Small mark 24 px to 47 px

Pixel mark 16 px favicon and app icon only

06

Typography and numerals

One drawn pen for the wordmark and numbers, one type family for words. The numbers have to read from the far baseline, so they get the most care.

Boss Fight

Archivo, Expanded Black. Headlines and name bars. Closest in weight and width to the drawn wordmark.

Every player gets one more life.

Archivo, Regular and Semibold. Everything you read: rosters, schedules, posts.

We picked each other.

Instrument Serif, Italic. Only for the hidden messages inside the jerseys.

Jersey numerals, drawn

Same centerline pen as the wordmark: 26 stroke, round terminals, closed 4. Cream fill, black keyline, gold ring on dark kits. Flat color only, no gradients.

Name bar

Two Bellamys rule. The generational suffix is part of the name bar: Gordon reads BELLAMY 4, Werten reads BELLAMY III 5. The suffix sits one word space after the name at the same cap height. Never initials, never first names.

Long names

Long-name rule. Up to 8 letters: straight, full width. 9 or more: condense to fit the name zone, then add a 10% arch if it still crowds. The cap height never drops below 1.25 in on an adult back.

Roster backs

The full roster, one pen, one name bar. Two Bellamys, told apart by the suffix.

07

Lockups

Six approved arrangements. If a layout needs something else, it uses one of these at a different size.

08

Misuse

Twelve ways to break the boss. Two of them are promises: no championship star until we win Sin City, and no USA marks, because we are a team with Team USA veterans, not a national team.

09

The boss, character sheet

The boss is the asset people will love and the one we can protect. Four moods and six costumes cover all twenty looks.

Costumes

Locked. Same body, same eyes, same smile in every world. Costumes sit on top; the crown is always visible, even pinned to a cap. Never stretched, never mean, never a real game character.

Guardrail

  • Never write "Vice City", anywhere, ever.
  • No Rockstar or GTA art, logos, lettering or characters. No pricedown-style type, no sunset-logo pastiche.
  • No in-game team names. Generic 1980s Miami only: neon script, pink and teal, Art Deco, palms, sunsets.
  • Use standard hot pink and teal inks unless Callie confirms fluorescent.

Trademark note

Short version: wear it proudly, check before selling.

Our own jerseysFree giveawaysSelling merch

Reported FINAL BOSS filings exist, including Class 25 (apparel) and Class 41 (entertainment and sporting activities); their current status is unverified. Wearing the name on our own team jerseys is low risk. Before selling anything, get a USPTO search and a lawyer's read. The boss ball character is the distinctive, protectable asset, so lead with it.
